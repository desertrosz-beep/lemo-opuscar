# CHAI RUN — treatment

80s Cel Anime, repainted for an Indian sunrise. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 58.5 s, YouTube Short.
Music: the user's own `chai_run_music_edit.mp3`, the only music. No voice. SFX only. Hinglish captions.

## 1. Structure

The user fixed the scene list and the hard sync points; the structure decisions below are about how to carry it.

1. **Loop-flashback (chosen).** Open on the finish, rewind the tape to 5 AM, play the ordinary days that built the runner, land on the same finish frame so the Short loops. The VHS rewind is the medium's own move (tape artefacts as punctuation), so the flashback is told *by the tape itself*.
2. A straight one-day journey: 5 AM to race, no cold open. Clear, but no hook in the first 3 s and no loop.
3. A countdown: the stair counter 0 → 300 as the spine of the whole film. Strong graphic idea, but it eats the race. Kept as a motif instead (counters on screen).

Why 1: it gives the hook in the first frame (slow-motion overtake), a question ("…ya ki?"), and an ending that is literally the opening repainted with the full story behind it.

## 2. Logline and arc

A chai boy who runs 300 stairs every morning with 12 full glasses doesn't know he's been training, until an old coach with a stopwatch does.

- Setup (0–17.9): the daily run, the work, the mockery. Momentum.
- Turn (17.9–25.4): the glasses shatter, the music drops out, the Coach's stopwatch says 2 minutes. Someone *saw*.
- Ending (25.4–58.5): montage, the start line as last place, the pistol, the overtakes, the same finish frame, a glass of chai at sunrise.

## 3. Benchmark (grammar only)

- Late-80s sports-anime OVA openings in general: held bust shots with light bands, radial speed lines, 2-frame impact frames, smears on the sprint, a hard freeze on the climax. Learn: how stillness (a held close-up) is used right before speed.
- 80s TV shampoo/athletics commercials shot on tape: warm halation, low sun, long shadows. Learn: sunrise as the colour model, the sun as the emissive source.
- Don't take: any character, team kit, logo, shot or title design from a real series.

## 4. Colour model: SUNRISE (replaces the style's neon night)

- Sky plate: deep violet top → rose → saffron → white-gold horizon line; airbrushed clouds with gold undersides. The sun is the emissive source (bloom, flare, halation red-orange). Long shadows, purple-rose.
- Basti: pastel walls (turquoise, pink, lime, ochre) catching gold on east faces, violet on west faces.
- Cel lit set "sunrise": lit side warm gold multiplied, shadow side rose-violet; skin keeps its own colour per set (warm brown, shadow plum-brown).
- Backlit set (silhouette + saffron rim): used at the finish line and the final glass raise.
- **Chhotu's accent:** the faded orange t-shirt; always staged against violet shadow or turquoise wall so it never sits on saffron sky (no orange-on-orange). The **chai glass amber** is the film's second motif: glasses are backlit (they glow on the emissive plate).
- Coach's accent: old blue track jacket, the coolest colour on screen; the steel stopwatch gets a sliding highlight.

## 5. Characters (model sheet first)

- **CHHOTU**, 12. Lean, egg-shaped 80s head scaled young (bigger eyes in proportion, short jaw), messy black hair in 6–7 sharp locks with dark-purple line and angel ring, almond eyes with determined thick upper lash, faded orange tee (lighter orange fill, pale wear patches), brown shorts, torn rubber chappals (blue strap) — barefoot in the race. Steel tray with 12 small glass cups (3×4), amber chai, steel shine.
- **COACH**, 60s. Long face, thick white mustache, short white hair, grey stubble shadow, heavy brows, old blue track jacket with two white stripes on the sleeve and a zip, steel stopwatch on a red cord. Quiet: emotion only in brows and lids (one wide-eye beat).
- Rich kids (2): shiny white-and-neon sneakers, generic sporty clothes, no brands.

## 9. Type and captions

Captions are on the tape, inside the CRT pass: heavy italic sans in a chrome sunrise gradient (violet → white horizon line → pink → gold) with a thick dark plum outline and a saffron outer glow, flicking on at 12 fps with a light sweep. Size ~96–120 px, centred at x ≈ 480 (the right ~140 px kept free for YouTube's buttons), between y ≈ 300 and 1300; nothing below y = 1536. Counters (☕ 12/12, stair counter) are an 80s sports-broadcast OSD in the top-left: a drawn cel chai glass instead of the emoji (no emoji font on tape; drawn icons match the style), the 👇 drawn as a cel pointing arrow. Hinglish exactly as given.

## 10. Native move

**Tape artefacts as punctuation**: the rewind at 2.46 is the hinge of the loop. Peak: **speed grammar** (radial lines + impact frames + smears) at the pistol and the overtakes, with the stairway repainted over the track — the training made visible.

## 11. Technique

Canvas 2D page with `render(t)`, cel shading (fill, hard shadow, hard highlight, coloured outline), characters on twos, camera on ones; painted plates generated once at load with an emissive pair; WebGL tape + CRT pass (bloom, halation, chroma bleed, scanlines, tracking noise for the rewind); captions composited inside the pass. SFX procedural with `core/audio/sfx.py`, mixed under the user's track, two-pass loudnorm −14 LUFS.
